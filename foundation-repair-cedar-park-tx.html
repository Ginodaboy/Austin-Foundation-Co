Foundation issues common in Cedar Park

Cedar Park's caliche-heavy soils can be particularly unpredictable — caliche doesn't absorb water the way clay does, which means moisture often pools above it rather than draining through, creating localized wet spots that contribute to uneven foundation movement.

The warning signs are the same regardless of neighborhood — diagonal cracks above doors and windows, floors that slope or feel springy, doors that stick or won't latch, and gaps appearing between walls and baseboards or ceilings. If you're noticing two or more of these, a free inspection is the right next step.

Diagonal wall cracks Typically radiating from door or window corners — usually the earliest sign of uneven settlement.
Sticking doors or windows The frame is racking out of square as the foundation shifts beneath it.
Sloping or bouncy floors A marble that rolls on its own is a clear sign of measurable foundation movement.
Gaps at baseboards or ceiling Indicates separation — the wall is pulling away from a surface it used to sit flush against.
